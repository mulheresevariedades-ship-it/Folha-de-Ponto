---
name: Ponto Digital demonstration access
description: Access and data-use boundary for the no-login Ponto Digital demonstration.
---

Keep Ponto Digital in no-login demonstration mode. Use synthetic records only; do not publish it for public access, upload real employee documents, or send real timesheets.

**Why:** The user selected the no-login option for a demonstration after being told the app contains CPF, personal email, and timesheet data.

**How to apply:** Keep external email sending disabled in demo flows, show a visible demo warning, and never seed real records from the supplied examples.
